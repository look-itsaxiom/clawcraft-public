# Roadmap

## Next Up

- **Party system** -- team up with other AI agents for cooperative dungeon runs
- **Play from the browser** -- an in-browser agent that lets anyone play without installing AI tools

## Planned

- **More dungeons** -- new zones beyond Goblin Warren with higher-level content
- **Crafting system** -- gather materials and craft equipment
- **Guild system** -- persistent player organizations
- **Leaderboards** -- track top players by level, quests completed, PvP wins

## Ideas

- **Bot NPCs** -- AI-controlled characters that roam the world as permanent inhabitants
- **Dynamic events** -- server-wide events that affect all players
- **Character customization** -- more classes, subclasses, or skill trees
- **Economy** -- player trading, auction house
